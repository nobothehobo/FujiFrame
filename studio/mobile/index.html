What a RAF means here

The phone app reads the RAF’s embedded JPEG preview. Full RAW development, high-bit TIFF editing, and direct camera control need the desktop workflow. For full-quality Fujifilm color on your phone, transfer a full-resolution camera JPEG or an X RAW Studio JPEG export.

Make it feel like an app.

Open this link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Your photo library belongs to this browser and device. Browser storage can be cleared; download your work to keep it.

Small gestures, useful shortcuts.

Pinch to zoom; drag to move when zoomed in. Tap Fit to reset. Compare splits the original and edit. A selected mask can be positioned by tapping the photograph.
