FROM CAMERA TO CREATIVE

Your Fujifilm workflow.

Two ways to keep your X-T5 photographs moving.

01 / EXACT CAMERA COLOR

X RAW Studio round-trip

Connect your X-T5 by USB. In the camera’s Network/USB settings, choose USB RAW CONV./BACKUP RESTORE and set USB power/communication to AUTO or POWER SUPPLY OFF/COMM ON.

Open the RAF in Fujifilm’s X RAW Studio, choose your film simulation, export in sRGB, then attach that JPEG or TIFF to its RAF here. Studio edits that rendered color.

Get X RAW Studio ↗
02 / AUTOMATIC TRANSFER

A folder that keeps up.

Use Fujifilm X Acquire or Tether App to transfer photographs to a folder on this computer. Studio watches that folder and imports completed files. You can also watch an X RAW Studio export folder.

Direct USB / Wi-Fi camera control is not implemented. Fujifilm’s SDK supports X-T5 transfer and basic controls, but excludes RAF conversion and carries specific licensing and warranty terms. No SDK binaries or Adobe profiles are bundled. SDK details ↗
READY FOR THE WORLD

Let it out.

A new copy. Exactly how you see it.

Exports are sRGB. Source EXIF and location metadata are excluded. Your original and its recipe metadata remain in the local catalog. Exporting 40MP photos can take time and several GB of memory.
