A note on resolution & metadata

Full-resolution framing adds pixels around your photo and never crops or scales the image itself. PNG avoids another round of JPEG compression. Browser decoding can convert color profiles to sRGB and reduce higher bit depths; this is not a byte-for-byte archival workflow. RAF / RAW and HEIC are not supported: choose the camera JPEG or an exported PNG.

Keep metadata copies standard EXIF (including camera settings, dates and GPS when present), basic XMP, and PNG text fields. EXIF orientation and image dimensions are updated. XMP is copied verbatim and may still describe the original dimensions. Legacy IPTC, extended XMP and WebP metadata are not copied. Apps such as Instagram may remove metadata later. Exclude omits all source metadata.

Large photos use band-based PNG encoding to avoid full-size canvas limits, but a full-resolution X-T5 photo can still exceed a phone browser’s memory limit. FujiFrame will report a failed export and let you choose a smaller copy; it never silently downsizes your full-resolution choice. For very large photos, a desktop browser is recommended.
